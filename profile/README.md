## Laboratorio Aclimu

Laboratorio Aclimu is a clinical laboratory in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Its Genomics Unit performs clinical exome and
gene panel sequencing, including hereditary cancer testing.

We share data and tools that arise from our quality work, so that other laboratories can check and improve their own
results.

### Resources

- [Recurrent NovaSeq X sequencing artifacts at clinically interpreted positions](https://github.com/Laboratorio-Aclimu/novaseqx-artifacts): per-allele list, exome-wide catalog and [lookup page](https://laboratorio-aclimu.github.io/novaseqx-artifacts/) (doi:[10.5281/zenodo.23956232](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23956232))
- `nsx_artifact_check`: read-level check of listed alleles in BAM/CRAM and VCF files
